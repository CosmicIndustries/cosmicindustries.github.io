04 / EPISTEMOLOGY

Evidence stays separated

Weak evidence remains queryable without being promoted. Computational predictions have their own provenance class.

L0

Primary science

Measured chemistry, experimental biology, genomics, metabolomics, toxicology and controlled assays.

L1

Reviews

Systematic reviews and syntheses linked back to primary studies.

L2

Ethnobotany

Field studies, historical records and documented cultural practice.

L3

Clinical observation

Case reports, pharmacovigilance, poisoning and adverse-event observations.

L4

Phenomenology

Community and first-person observations, retained as situated reports.

L5

Anecdote

Weak or unsourced reports remain quarantined from scientific inference.

Prediction ≠ experiment. Docking, inferred mechanisms and ML associations are stored as prediction provenance and cannot silently become L0.
