02 / PHYLLOTAXIS

Meristem geometry, not a decorative label

Phyllotaxis records the spatial and temporal pattern of organ initiation. MATERIA keeps the measurable geometry separate from later interpretation.

Observed pattern

Parameters MATERIA tracks

FIELDMEANING
Divergence angle αAngle between successive lateral organs at the SAM.
Plastochron TTime between successive organ initiation events.
Plastochron ratio RRadial distance ratio between successive primordia.
Rise / radial growthAxial or radial displacement associated with successive organs.
ParastichiesVisible spiral families joining neighboring organs.
JugacyNumber of organs initiated per cycle/node.
ChiralityClockwise/counterclockwise handedness where applicable.
Generative helixInitiation-order trajectory of successive organs.
Meristem geometrySAM radius, generative annulus and primordium positions when measured.
TransitionChange of phyllotactic regime during development.
Important: Fibonacci numbers and the golden angle are descriptors that can emerge in some spiral systems; they are not universal identifiers of phyllotaxis.
