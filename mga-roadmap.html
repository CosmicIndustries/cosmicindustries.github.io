SSI / SSDI eligibility review
SSI depends on qualifying disability and limited income/resources. SSDI depends on qualifying disability and sufficient work credits. Having a diagnosis or currently having no income does not itself establish eligibility. Screen the work-credit history and SSA medical criteria before counting either program.
