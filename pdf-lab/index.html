Local research engine

The local worker downloads permitted PDFs, extracts text page by page, and indexes documents in SQLite FTS5. Catalog search uses Porter stemming and BM25 relevance ranking; grep uses concurrent, memoized page extraction and ripgrep when installed.

Research workflow & boundaries

1. Discover school-domain URLs with *.edu/**/*.pdf. 2. Select candidates and download/index them locally. 3. Search the catalog or run regex grep across extracted text. PDFs stay on the worker machine. Common Crawl, Arquivo.pt, and Wayback are historical, incomplete indexes—not live inventories. Their URL candidates can seed bounded host/path fuzzing. Crawling respects robots.txt and does not bypass authentication or access controls.
