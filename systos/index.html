03 / RELATION ENGINE

Links are data, not decoration.

Relationships now open as inspectable objects. SYSTOS distinguishes direct structural relations, mechanistic/causal candidates, and HRR correlation. Correlation or semantic proximity never becomes proof of causation.

CAUSATION / MECHANISM

Trace proposed effects

Relations such as supports, can_affect, enables, regulates, governs, and transmits can be inspected as mechanistic candidates. Each edge retains confidence, notes, temporal scope, and provenance.

CAUSAL CLAIM ≠ CAUSAL PROOF
CORRELATION / ASSOCIATION

Measure semantic proximity

D3 HRR vectors can be compared with cosine similarity to surface nearby concepts. This is an associative retrieval signal—not a historical claim that two systems caused one another.

HRR SIMILARITY · NON-CAUSAL
04 / EVIDENCE

No sanitized history.

SYSTOS records documented corruption and abuse without assuming that an allegation is a fact. Evidence, counterclaims, uncertainty, and provenance remain visible.

Corruption is a data object.
Bribery, nepotism, patronage, fraud, embezzlement, extortion, regulatory capture, state capture, conflicts of interest, coercive extraction, information suppression, and institutional self-dealing can all be modeled as mechanisms operating inside a system. False or disputed accusations are modeled separately rather than silently converted into facts.

Claim

What is being asserted, by whom, when, and about which system state?

Evidence

Primary sources, archaeology, records, testimony, datasets, scholarship, and other traceable evidence.

Counterclaim

Competing interpretations remain attached to the record instead of being erased.
