03 / EVIDENCE

No sanitized history.

SYSTOS records documented corruption and abuse without assuming that an allegation is a fact. Evidence, counterclaims, uncertainty, and provenance remain visible.

Corruption is a data object.
Bribery, nepotism, patronage, fraud, embezzlement, extortion, regulatory capture, state capture, conflicts of interest, coercive extraction, information suppression, and institutional self-dealing can all be modeled as mechanisms operating inside a system. False or disputed accusations are modeled separately rather than silently converted into facts.

Claim

What is being asserted, by whom, when, and about which system state?

Evidence

Primary sources, archaeology, records, testimony, datasets, scholarship, and other traceable evidence.

Counterclaim

Competing interpretations remain attached to the record instead of being erased.
