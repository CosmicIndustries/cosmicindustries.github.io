## Cross-correlation highlights

- **JASMINE ↔ atmospheric microplastics:** both are reproducible computational/scientific data-lineage objects where future reanalysis depends on preserving inputs, assumptions and propagated uncertainty.
- **Pondaung fossils ↔ Hydrangea taxonomy:** both demonstrate the same Fort Knowledge pattern: preserve primary evidence separately from versioned taxonomic interpretation.
- **iNaturalist Marine ↔ NOAA marine survey:** complementary observation designs can be compared for spatial coverage and sampling bias without treating citizen science and standardized survey effort as equivalent.
- **Atmospheric microplastics ↔ low-cost sensors:** model-output and measurement-uncertainty layers can eventually be compared, but simulation must remain distinct from observation.
- **RKI influenza ↔ COVID hospitalization/death series:** national surveillance streams can be linked by reporting architecture and temporal structure while preserving disease-specific case definitions, denominators, delays and nowcast methodology.
- **Bee–plant interactions ↔ occurrence archives:** taxonomic/geographic occurrence context can strengthen interaction-network interpretation, but occurrence alone does not establish an interaction.

## Integrity / preservation findings

- **Source state:** today's strongest new state is `NEW_VERSION` / `DAILY_VERSION`, not merely `NEW_DATA`. Versioned datasets such as the marine iNaturalist archive and RKI surveillance series should remain separate immutable observations with explicit version lineage.
- **Model vs observation:** atmospheric microplastic simulations are stored as model outputs, not measurements.
- **Quality flags:** the OBIS marine archive explicitly reports missing/invalid coordinates and other flags; those are preserved rather than used to discard the dataset. citeturn0search12
- **Taxonomic identity:** new-species records remain linked to source treatments and specimen evidence; registration is not treated as independent scientific validation.
- **Surveillance semantics:** RKI datasets are administrative/epidemiological reporting products. Reported cases, hospitalizations, deaths and nowcasts are not interchangeable quantities. citeturn1search23turn1search27turn1search26
