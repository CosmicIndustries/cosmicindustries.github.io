## Integrity notes

- GBIF registration is treated as a discovery/provenance layer, not as proof of every scientific claim in the underlying publication.
- Citizen-science records are kept separate from representativeness or causal claims.
- Quality flags such as missing depth fields are preserved as metadata-quality observations rather than used to discard an entire dataset.
- Historical field terminology and modern reinterpretations remain separate objects.
