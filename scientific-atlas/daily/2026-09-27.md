### Semantic-integrity controls

Today's package explicitly preserves:

- original vs normalized representations;
- specimen vs occurrence record;
- physical collection vs digital surrogate;
- historical publication vs OCR/extracted text;
- instrument observation vs processed derivative;
- source registry vs underlying scientific dataset;
- version change vs overwrite;
- duplicate collection series vs duplicate scientific evidence;
- unresolved identity vs inferred identity.

The Mozambique archive is particularly useful because it preserves 45 unresolved publication identities instead of forcing metadata resolution.

This is more durable than preserving a current URL alone.
