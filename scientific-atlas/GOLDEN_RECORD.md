# Fort Knowledge — Scientific Data Atlas Golden Record

**Record status:** GOLDEN RECORD  
**Recorded:** 2026-09-27  
**Repository:** CosmicIndustries/cosmicindustries.github.io  
**System:** Fort Knowledge / Scientific Data Atlas

## Purpose

This record establishes the current Atlas architecture and operating principles as a preserved baseline. It is a reference point for future expansion, not a replacement for the underlying evidence corpus.

## Golden-state principles

1. **Continuous evidence collection** — discover new, obscure, overlooked, migrated, changed, archived, endangered, recovered, and newly digitized scientific evidence.
2. **Append-only knowledge growth** — new evidence and interpretations are added without silently rewriting prior evidence identities.
3. **Provenance preservation** — retain source identity, canonical identifiers, repository lineage, versions, access state, verification dates, and discovery context.
4. **Source-state distinction** — distinguish DISCOVERED, CHANGED, MOVED, ARCHIVED, RETIRED, PARTIAL, ACCESS_RESTRICTED, MIRROR_ONLY, DEAD, and recovered states where applicable.
5. **Semantic integrity** — preserve aliases, historical terminology, language identity, transliteration, attribution uncertainty, narrative framing, corrections, contradictions, and fictionalization separately from factual conclusions.
6. **Evidence graph** — maintain typed STRUCTURAL, PROVENANCE, EVIDENTIAL, ANALOGICAL, CORRELATIONAL, and CAUSAL relationships.
7. **Causal discipline** — correlation, chronology, analogy, or shared metadata do not become causal claims without explicit supporting evidence, mechanism, scope, alternatives/confounders, and limitations.
8. **Evidence hierarchy** — prioritize primary datasets, peer-reviewed evidence, systematic reviews, authoritative catalogs, taxonomic resources, repositories, and other source-backed records while preserving lower-level discovery candidates as such.
9. **Visual integrity** — Atlas visualizations, encyclopedia articles, graphs, timelines, and infographics must be generated from recorded fields or explicitly labeled derived calculations; missing facts are never invented for presentation.
10. **Long-horizon preservation** — the website is an interface to the evidence system; preservation packages, provenance, fixity, replication, migration, and recovery remain the durable objective.

## Current interface baseline

The Atlas currently provides:

- searchable merged evidence catalog
- Atlas record filtering
- searchable Evidence Graph
- relationship-family filtering
- encyclopedia entity search
- full encyclopedia article pages
- evidence relationship maps
- related-article navigation
- provenance and evidence-profile panels
- evidence timelines where source metadata supports them
- fullscreen **Atlas** view
- fullscreen **Evidence Graph** view

## Non-destructive operating rule

Diagnostics and maintenance are evidence-first and read-only by default. Existing evidence identities, learned information, configurations, relationships, contradictions, and historical states are not silently corrected, overwritten, or reconciled.

When a later source conflicts with an earlier record, preserve both states and document the relationship.

## Golden-record interpretation

This document marks a **known-good architectural and methodological baseline as of 2026-09-27**. It does not certify the truth of every indexed scientific claim. Individual evidence objects retain their own provenance, epistemic status, verification date, source state, uncertainty, and limitations.

Future Atlas updates should extend this record's lineage rather than erase the historical state it represents.
