# Encyclopedia + Knowledge Graph Layer

Fort Knowledge now has a second layer above the evidence catalog.

## Model

Search -> Entity -> Article -> Relationship -> Evidence -> Source

The encyclopedia is a rendering/index layer, not the authoritative archive. Evidence records remain source objects. Articles and graph relationships are derived views with provenance.

## Epistemic contract

- ASSERTED_RECORD_METADATA — directly represented by a curated evidence record.
- DERIVED_INDEX — mechanically derived from existing records.
- OBSERVED_RELATIONSHIP — a documented relationship between evidence objects; not causation.
- CAUSAL_CLAIM — reserved for relationships whose causal basis, mechanism, scope, alternatives, and supporting evidence are explicitly recorded.
- HYPOTHESIS — proposed explanation awaiting sufficient evidence.
- CONTRADICTORY — competing claims/evidence are retained rather than reconciled silently.

## Relationship families

STRUCTURAL, PROVENANCE, EVIDENTIAL, ANALOGICAL, CORRELATIONAL, CAUSAL

A relationship may be highly useful without being causal. Analogy and archival lineage are intentionally not rendered as correlation.

## Causal record requirements

A future causal edge must carry: causal basis; mechanism; supporting evidence identifiers; alternatives/confounders considered; scope/population; limitations; verification date.

No causal edge is inferred merely because two records co-occur, correlate, or appear sequentially.

## Preservation rule

New interpretations append new records/edges. Existing evidence identities and prior relationships are never silently overwritten.
