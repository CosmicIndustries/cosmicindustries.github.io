Core principles

Evidence is not interpretation.Preserve the object, its provenance, its claims, and later interpretations as separate layers.
A dead URL is not proof of suppression.Track moved, retired, archived, partial, inaccessible, migrated, and genuinely lost sources separately.
Never overwrite the historical object.Migrations and modern interpretations are new derivatives linked to the original.
Language is a rendering layer.Words change. Meaning is anchored through relationships, observations, symbols, context, and provenance.
The guardian is temporary.The preservation package must remain recoverable even when this website, organization, or technology does not.
Access is not a moral score.Ordinary knowledge is discoverable by default; explicit rights, privacy, safety, and integrity constraints are handled separately.

Snapshot: 2026-09-21 · Fort Knowledge is a preservation architecture in development. OAIS, PREMIS, BagIt, WARC, PROV, and related standards are design references, not certification claims.
