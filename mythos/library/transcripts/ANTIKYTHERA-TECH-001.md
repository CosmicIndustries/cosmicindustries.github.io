# ANTIKYTHERA-TECH-001 — Digital Transcript

**Collection:** Recovered Knowledge / Ancient Technology  
**Era:** Hellenistic Mediterranean · c. 2nd century BCE  
**Evidence state:** Corroborated

## Abstract
The Antikythera Mechanism is a geared astronomical instrument whose functions can be reconstructed from surviving fragments, inscriptions and imaging.

## Demonstrated capabilities
- Mechanical representation of astronomical cycles.
- Lunar and solar calendrical relationships.
- Eclipse-cycle calculation.
- Sophisticated geared transmission.

## What is *not* established
The mechanism does not by itself demonstrate:
- a lost industrial civilization,
- extraterrestrial engineering,
- universal ancient technological superiority.

## Why it matters
This is the ideal MYTHOS pattern for “mysterious technology”:
**unusual artifact → surviving physical evidence → reconstruction → experimentally testable capability.**
