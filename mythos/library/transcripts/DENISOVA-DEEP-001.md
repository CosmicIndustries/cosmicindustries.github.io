# DENISOVA-DEEP-001 — Digital Transcript

**Collection:** Deep Time / Hominin Memory  
**Era:** Middle Pleistocene → Late Pleistocene  
**Evidence state:** Corroborated for occupation and interaction; undetermined for narrative transmission

## Abstract
Denisova Cave preserves evidence for multiple human lineages, including Denisovans, Neanderthals and modern humans. Genomic evidence also documents a first-generation Neanderthal–Denisovan offspring.

## Core observations
1. Multiple human populations occupied the same site across long intervals.
2. Interbreeding between human lineages is directly demonstrated genetically.
3. Symbolic behavior exists in several Middle/Late Pleistocene contexts.
4. No specific oral tradition can currently be traced to a Neanderthal or Denisovan source.

## Memory hypothesis
**H1:** later human traditions preserve a specific Neanderthal/Denisovan narrative.

**Current status:** Undetermined.

### Required evidence
- A securely dated later tradition.
- A plausible transmission bridge.
- Content sufficiently specific to exceed generic convergent motifs.
- Independent archaeological or environmental correlation.

### Chronology warning
The main Holocene postglacial sea-level pulse occurred after Neanderthals disappeared. Therefore “Neanderthals remembered the postglacial flood” fails a direct chronology test.
