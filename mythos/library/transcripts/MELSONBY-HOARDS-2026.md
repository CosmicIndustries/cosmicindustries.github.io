# MELSONBY-HOARDS-2026 — Digital Transcript

**Collection:** Iron Age Britain / Technology
**Evidence state:** Corroborated

## Abstract
The Melsonby Hoards constitute an unusually large Late Iron Age metalwork deposit in Britain. Radiocarbon dating places deposition around 40 BCE–40 CE. The assemblage contains nearly 950 objects, including vehicle components, iron tyres, horse harness, weapons and feasting equipment.

## Claim decomposition
- Complex wheeled vehicles existed in Late Iron Age Britain: **Corroborated**.
- Vehicle technology can be reconstructed from the surviving components: **Strongly supported**.
- The deposit represents ordinary vehicle maintenance waste: **Not established**.
- The assemblage proves Roman technological origin: **Contradicted by chronology/context**.

## Counterevidence / limits
The objects were deliberately deposited and dismantled. Their deposition history and social meaning require interpretation; component survival does not provide a complete vehicle manual.

## Historical meaning
This is a model case for MYTHOS recovered-technology records: physical parts → chronology → reconstruction → experiment → bounded inference.
