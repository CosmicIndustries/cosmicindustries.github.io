# DENISOVAN-SW-CHINA-2026 — Digital Transcript

**Collection:** Deep Time / Archaic Humans
**Evidence state:** Corroborated for identification; behavior reconstructed cautiously

## Abstract
September 2026 Nature research identified multiple Denisovan remains in Southwest China using ancient proteins and associated archaeological evidence. A companion study examined Denisovan subsistence and animal exploitation at Baishiya Karst Cave.

## Claim decomposition
- Denisovans existed in Southwest China: **Corroborated**.
- Denisovan remains can be identified using palaeoproteomics where DNA is limited: **Corroborated**.
- Denisovans had a geographically broad Asian distribution: **Strongly supported**, while exact population structure remains under study.
- Every artifact at the site was made by Denisovans: **Not established**.

## Counterevidence / limits
Protein identification is powerful but does not automatically assign every associated artifact or occupation episode to the identified hominin population.

## Historical meaning
The Denisovan record is shifting from a single exceptional cave toward a distributed East Asian archaeological record. That changes the questions available to MYTHOS: migration, ecology, subsistence and interaction become testable dimensions rather than speculative extensions.
