# MATERIA — Open Ethnobotanical Research Atlas

A public-facing research atlas for medicinal plants, fungi and entheogens.

## Evidence model

The project deliberately separates:

- primary science
- systematic reviews
- ethnobotany / traditional knowledge
- clinical observations
- community phenomenology (including Shroomery and Erowid/Trip Reports)
- individual anecdotes

**No layer is silently promoted into another.**

"Insufficient clinical evidence" does not mean "the experience did not happen." It means clinical evidence is insufficient.

## Safety

This is a research/education index, not a dosing guide or treatment recommendation. High-risk substances should have interaction, toxicology, legal-status and uncertainty fields prominently represented.
