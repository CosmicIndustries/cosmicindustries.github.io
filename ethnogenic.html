Corpus architecture: taxonomy is queried from the current Catalogue of Life release; natural-product chemistry is linked to NP Atlas/LOTUS rather than copied into a single Pages payload. This keeps the atlas scalable to hundreds of thousands of taxa and tens of thousands of natural products.
LOTUS chemistry bridge: search the live natural-products corpus by organism, molecule name, LOTUS ID, InChI, formula or structure. LOTUS currently exposes 276,518 natural products and its records include producing-organism taxonomy and references.
