A living institution for the five pillars: matter, knowledge, story, systems, and culture. Each pillar is a different view into the same interconnected human record.

01 / THE FIVE PILLARS

Five pillars. One corpus.

The pillars are not doors to be opened or closed. They are persistent foundations of the collection, and an artifact, idea, practice, or system may exist across several at once.

PILLAR 01
⌘

Plants, fungi, organisms, substances, compounds, preparations, uses, toxicity, traditional knowledge, and the material world.

02 / CENTRAL ATRIUM

The semantic spine

The Museum is a presentation layer over a deeper semantic archive.

THE COLLECTION MODEL

Nothing exists alone.

A medicinal plant can be MATERIA, appear in traditional knowledge, enter a ritual in MYTHOS, participate in a trade system in SYSTOS, and become part of CULTURA. FORTKNOWLEDGE records what is known, how it became known, and where uncertainty remains.

The underlying archive is designed for semantic association rather than a rigid hierarchy. Human-readable pillars are projections of the corpus, not its limits.

04 / MUSEUM CHARTER

How the collection behaves.

Preserve contextRecords retain time, place, language, culture, and provenance.
Separate claim from factEvidence, uncertainty, interpretation, and disagreement remain visible.
Do not sanitizeCorruption, violence, exploitation, failure, resistance, and contradiction can be archived as data.
Connect, don't flattenSemantic relationships reveal connections without pretending proximity proves causation.
