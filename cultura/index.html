Human culture,
as a living graph.

A semantic atlas of traditions, languages, philosophies, religions, artistic movements, ideologies, rebellions, revivals, diasporas, institutions, and the exchanges between them.

Culture is not treated as a ranking. CULTURA separates description, interpretation, and evidence so disagreement can remain visible instead of being flattened into a single narrative.

04 / METHOD

Evidence before narrative

DESCRIPTION

What is documented?

Observable practices, artifacts, texts, institutions, events, languages, material remains, recorded statements, and other evidence.

INTERPRETATION

Who says what it means?

Participant accounts, historians, anthropologists, archaeologists, philosophers, political theorists, critics, and competing schools of scholarship remain attributed.

PROVENANCE

Where did the claim come from?

Each claim can eventually point to primary sources, datasets, scholarship, archival material, archaeological evidence, and confidence or dispute metadata.
